## Z. `index.html` Is Two Sections And A Clip, And 「今日推薦」 Took The Other Eight
The homepage used to be nine content sections. It is now a hero and five cards, and everything else moved to `today.html`, which is the first row under 圖鑑. This section records the three decisions that are not derivable from the code, because all three are choices someone could re-litigate from scratch.

- **THE SCRUB WRITES `currentTime` AND NEVER CALLS `play()`.** The hero is a 240vh track whose sticky layer holds one generation's 10 s clip, and the reader's progress through the track is written straight into `video.currentTime`. Autoplay would give the reader a loop they did not ask for and could not stop; scrub gives them a position that is a function of where they are. **Scrolling UP plays the clip backwards**, and that falls out of the mechanism rather than being a feature — `currentTime` is a seek, and a seek to a smaller number goes to a smaller number. A play/pause transport cannot do it without a second implementation of time.
- **PROGRESS DIVIDES BY `rect.height - innerHeight`, AND THE SECOND TERM IS THE WHOLE CLAIM.** Drop it and the denominator is the entire track, progress tops out at 1 − 1/2.4 = **0.583**, and the last 42% of the clip is unreachable **while everything still looks correct**. Measured through the browser checker: with the term, `currentTime` equals `progress × duration` to a worst error of **0.000 s** across five stops, and the clip reaches **10.00 s of 10 s**.
- **BOTH ENDS OF THE CLIP ARE BLANK, AND THAT IS DECLARED RATHER THAN FIXED — BUT THE PAGE DOES NOT LAND ON ONE OF THEM, AND IT USED TO BE DOCUMENTED AS IF IT DID.** `make_hero_video.py` builds the roster across the clip's first 65% and fades it back out over the last 13%, so frame 0 and frame 299 both contain no sprites. **Measured offline off the shipped bytes in the checker's own left band: frame 0 = 0.00% ink, frame 299 = 0.00%, frame 195 = 23.01%.** **The author was shown this and chose to keep the full clip** (「維持 0%→100%」) over re-mapping the scrub onto the wave, which is the one-line alternative.
  - **THE 0% STOP HAS TWO LEGITIMATE ANSWERS AND THE PAGE RACES BETWEEN THEM.** **LANDING** on a fresh load: `MIN_SEEK_DELTA` makes `seek()` refuse a write within one frame of where it already is, so at the top of the track nothing is written, no frame is presented, and the compositor is still showing the **poster** — the COMPLETED roster, cut at `POSTER_FRACTION` 0.65, measured 22.75% in that band. **ARRIVING** back from 30%: currentTime was at 3.0s, so the write happens, frame 0 is presented, and the band reads 0.00%. **`check_home_hero_browser.mjs` asserted the poster, passed once, and went red on the next run** — the two answers differ by 23 points of ink, so no tolerance could have held both. **Expect this shape whenever a state is reached by two routes and the assertion names one of them.**
  - **SO THE SENTENCE 「A READER THEREFORE LANDS ON AN EMPTY HERO AND SCROLLS OFF ONE」 WAS WRONG, AND IT WAS WRONG IN THE READER'S FAVOUR.** They land on a finished roster, and fade out to the blank field at the top once they have scrolled. What is asserted now is the part that is not a race — the clip is parked on frame 0 at 0% — and the part that carries the risk, which nothing asserted for as long as this file existed: **that arriving back at the top from 30% DOES reach the blank frame 0** (0.00% ink). `MIN_SEEK_DELTA` creeping up to a second, or `seek()` gaining an early return, would park the reader on the poster however far they scrolled, and every other assertion in the file would still pass — the identity, the monotonicity, the middle stops.
  - **THE OLD FAILURE MESSAGE NAMED A CAUSE ITS OWN RUN HAD JUST DISPROVED**: 「the scrub is no longer 0 -> 1」, printed on a run where the identity assertion *above* reported `currentTime == progress × duration` to a worst error of **0.000s**. The scrub was always 0 → 1. A message that names a cause its own run has contradicted is worse than none. §3.G9's rule one section over.
- **THE POSTER IS NOT A PRE-PLAY FLASH.** Nothing calls `play()`, so `poster` is not transient: until the first seek it **is** what is on screen. One file covers the ~200 ms before metadata, a reduced-motion reader (frozen on the *same frame* the poster was cut from — `POSTER_FRACTION` is read out of `make_hero_video.py`'s `PHASE["build_end"]` rather than written down, because 0.65 in two places is two places to be wrong and both still produce a valid number), and a load that never arrives. There is no second fallback asset and no metadata timeout: a clip still downloading shows its poster, and a deadline would discard a download that is 90% done.
- **THE DAY IS ONE IMPLEMENTATION, TWO CALLERS.** `hero-gen.js` **imports `taipeiDateKey` from `daily-pick.js`** rather than computing a date. Two date keys computed two ways is how the homepage says one generation and another Pokémon for four hours. **THE EPOCH IS IN THE FUTURE AND THE WRAP IS THE POINT**: `GEN_ONE_DATE` is 2026-10-18 and the day this shipped was 2026-10-16, so `dayOffset` wraps **in both directions** — 10-16 is the eighth generation, 10-17 the ninth, 10-18 the first — and every date in the calendar maps to exactly one of the nine with no special case for the gap. A JS `%` keeps the dividend's sign, so `((o % 9) + 9) % 9` is load-bearing: without the inner `+9` a pre-epoch date indexes off the end of the array and yields `undefined`, which renders as generation 1 with nothing logged.
- **THE HERO IS A DARK BAND IN LIGHT THEME, ON PURPOSE.** The clip bakes `--bg-primary` and has no alpha channel, so there is nothing to blend and the hero cannot follow the theme. **The scrim therefore fades to the clip's OWN background (`#0b1020`) and not to `--bg-primary`** — fading to the theme's token means the bottom of a dark clip dissolves into a white page in light theme, which reads as a rendering fault. What is left is a deliberate edge. The author's call.
- **THE PANEL IS DARK IN BOTH THEMES, WITH ITS OWN ACCENT.** `--accent-gold` is crimson in light theme and measures **1.63:1** on this panel, so the hero declares `--home-hero-accent: #f5c542` rather than reaching for the shared token. The fill is **`rgba(6, 9, 20, 0.7)`** and the h1 reads at **4.75:1** against the panel composited over **pure white** — the bound no frame can exceed, and the assertion — and **8.44–9.40:1** from decoded pixels across the wave.
- **THE PANEL'S ALPHA IS A FLOOR DERIVED FROM WCAG, NOT A TASTE, and it was 0.8 until a reader asked to see through it.** A sprite can be any colour up to pure white, so the ground behind the title can be no darker than the fill composited over white — a figure depending on no frame and no scroll position, which is why `check_home_hero_browser.mjs`'s own docblock calls it unfoolable and why `check_home_hero.mjs` can compute it from the two declarations. Measured across the whole panel: **0.85 → h1 8.36 · 0.80 → 6.98 · 0.75 → 5.73 · 0.70 → 4.73 · 0.65 → 3.93 ✗ · 0.60 → 3.27 ✗ · 0.50 → 2.30 ✗ · 0.40 → 1.67 ✗.** **0.65 is the first value under 4.5:1, so 0.70 is the most transparent this panel can be** — the request was for 0.5, which is 2.30:1, and the primary button is unaffected either way because it paints the accent at full alpha (a fixed 11.30:1). **The bound is computed, never restated**, so the floor cannot drift away from WCAG the way a written-down `0.7` could. **Expect this shape anywhere a translucency is a design lever: it is also an accessibility measurement, and the two questions have one answer.**
- **AND ALPHA ALONE COULD NOT DO IT — THE BLUR WAS WHAT WAS HIDING THE SPRITES.** At 0.8 with `blur(10px)` the panel is a colour wash: nothing of a Pokémon's shape survives it, so dropping the alpha to 0.7 moved `rgb(56,58,67)` to `rgb(81,83,91)` and bought a *slightly lighter wash*. **`backdrop-filter: blur(3px)`, and the blur costs no contrast at all**, which is why it is the safer of the two levers and why both were pulled together. **And a blur is invisible to every contrast assertion, so it carries its own**: `check_home_hero.mjs` reads the radius out of its own declaration and requires ≤3px, because a checker that only guards what it can measure lets this go back to 100px silently.
- **THE STYLESHEET PARSING IS NOW ASSERTED, AND IT IS HERE BECAUSE IT DID NOT.** Every rule-shaped assertion in `check_home_hero.mjs` is a **regex over the `<style>` block's text**, and a regex cannot tell valid CSS from a broken one. Adding the alpha guard proved it: a comment in `index.html` was closed one delimiter early, the orphaned prose became live CSS, and the browser **discarded the rest of the block** — the panel lost its fill (computed alpha **0**), the h1 lost its accent and inherited `--text-primary`, and the hero rendered unstyled **while all 96 checks stayed green**, because every one of them was reading text the browser had already thrown away. **Then this file's own docblock reproduced it by naming the closing delimiter inside itself**, since a CSS comment does not nest. So the guard is a **count of comment openers against closers**, which is the one shape in a stylesheet that can silently truncate everything after it, and it cannot be satisfied vacuously — there is no reading of this page in which the two counts differ. **Expect this shape whenever a check reads a file as text: a rule can be present, correctly spelled, and not exist.**
- **THE PANEL IS A TITLE CARD — h1, the two CTAs and the scroll hint, and nothing else.** It was 760 of `max-width` and a measured **736×436 at 1280×900 — 27.9% of the viewport**, dead centre over a full-bleed roster grid; now **340×238, 7.0%**. **The phone was the worse case and is why this was worth doing: 350×421 at 390×844, 44.8% of the screen, is now 262×207, 16.5%.** The lede, 已更新至第 9 世代 and the three counts live in `.home-intro`, between the track and the feature grid. Three things that are decisions rather than code:
  - **`max-width: 760px` WAS ALREADY NOT BINDING, and is not binding now — the cap that decides this panel's width is the lede's `max-width: 660px`, which is what took it to 736.** With the lede gone the panel shrink-to-fits its widest child, the 42px h1 at 264px, and 760 binds only above ~300% browser zoom (where the title alone is 756px). **Expect this shape whenever a `max-width` on a shrink-to-fit flex item starts reading as dead weight** — the honest statement is that it is now a zoom guard, and it was a no-op before this change for a reason nobody could see from the stylesheet.
  - **THE SCROLL HINT STAYS IN THE PANEL** for two separate reasons: 「向下捲動播放」 is a claim about the *clip*, so moving it below the track makes it describe something the reader can no longer see; and it inherits the panel's legibility instead of needing a contrast surface of its own. It is the reason `#f5c542` and the 34px of padding above the h1 are **untouched** — which is why `check_home_hero_browser.mjs` needed no edit when the fill went from 0.8 to 0.7. The panel has since moved twice: narrowing it took the decoded pixels from 8.43–9.15:1 to **11.40–11.64:1** (the crop is the panel's padding strip above the title, and a 270px strip is less likely to contain a bright sprite than a 776px one), and dropping the fill to 0.7 with a 3px blur took them to **8.44–9.40:1**. The analytic bound followed the alpha down, 6.98:1 → **4.75:1**, and that is the floor.
  - **THE INTRO BLOCK IS BELOW A 240vh TRACK, so it lands at y=2215 on a 3030px page.** h1 + the two ways out are above the fold and the sentence saying what the site IS comes after the thing it is about. That is the two-act page, and it is why nothing above the fold got quieter. The document grew 3030 → 3263px at 1280×900 and 4216 → 4437 at 390×844.
- **THE THREE COUNTS ARE CLAIMS ABOUT OTHER PAGES AND ARE RE-DERIVED.** 1,025 comes from `pokemon_data.json`'s length, 9 from `GENERATION_COUNT`, 6 from `sorting-algorithms.js`'s `ALGO_IDS`. All three are hand-written markup and all three go stale quietly otherwise — the same shape as the four 精選系列 counts that drifted behind a 敬請期待 badge behind `check_categories.mjs`.

**MOVING TEXT OUT OF THE PANEL IS A REWRITE, NOT A MOVE — because the panel's colours are not the page's colours.** `#ffffff`, `#f4f6fc`, `#cfd6e8` and `#d7dbee` are tuned for `rgba(6,9,20,0.8)`; on the page's own `--bg-primary` they measure **~1.05:1** in light theme, so the paragraph and the three counts would have been elements that exist and are invisible. Every one became a token (`--text-primary` / `--text-secondary`), and `rgba(255,255,255,0.14)` on `.home-hero-stats` became `var(--hairline)` — a white 14% rule is not a subtle border on a white page, it is **no border**, and the numbers read as unattached. **Expect this shape anywhere a hardcoded colour crosses from an opaque local surface to the page's theme, and remember that no dark-theme screenshot can answer it.**

**`text-align: center` ON `.home-intro` IS DECLARED, NOT INHERITED, and the reason is the cheapest defect in the change.** `.home-hero-lede` used to centre itself off `.home-hero-sticky { text-align: center }` — one ancestor up, through a class nobody reads when looking at the lede's own rule. Moving the block out removed that ancestor, and the failure mode is not a red line: every rule still exists, no declaration is invalid, nothing throws. The paragraph is simply left-aligned under a centred title. **Expect this shape on any element that moves out of a container whose layout properties it was reading by inheritance** — the assertion is a `text-align` declaration on `.home-intro` and not "something centres it", because an inherited `center` satisfies the latter and breaks again the moment the block moves.

**AND `at()` IN `check_home_hero.mjs` WAS PROVING THE STYLESHEET'S ORDER WHILE ITS LABEL CLAIMED THE MARKUP'S.** The three hero selectors each appear twice — once as a rule inside `<style>` and once as an attribute — and the stylesheet comes first, so `htmlCode.indexOf` returned CSS offsets. The assertion passed **by coincidence**: both lists happen to read media, scrim, content. Reorder the markup and it stayed green; move a declaration and it went red for a reason with nothing to do with the page. It now slices past `</style>` first. **A check that reports the right verdict about the wrong thing is worse than no check, because a reader stops looking at it** — §3.J's `.cache` holding keys the manifest no longer has, one layer down. The same fix is why the new panel-contents assertion reads a block delimited on `\n {8}</div>` rather than the first `</div>`: `.home-hero-cta` closes at ten spaces, so the lazier delimiter stops inside the panel and reports the CTA's children (§3.P's second vacuous shape, written by the person who had just recorded it).

**THREE MEASUREMENT BUGS THIS COST, ALL OF WHICH REPORTED A CONFIDENT NUMBER** — the shape §3.G5 names, arrived at from a third direction.

- **`Page.captureScreenshot`'s `clip` is in PAGE coordinates, not viewport.** It is right at `scrollY === 0` and silently wrong the moment the page moves. Measured before the fix: the left band read **22.4% ink at 25% of the track and 0.0% at 75%**, which looked exactly like a hero showing the wrong frames and was a crop landing on the document's own header — the band image came back with `.header` drawn through the middle of it.
- **A BRIGHTEST-PIXEL SEARCH INSIDE A BOX CONTAINING THE TEXT FINDS ONLY THE TEXT.** The first contrast measurement cropped the whole panel and took its brightest pixel as the "worst case ground"; `#f5c542` is L=241, so the verdict was the title against itself at **1.44:1** on a panel that composites to 6.97:1 (the alpha at the time; 4.75:1 now). The crop is now the padding directly above the title, **inset by the corner radius** — the rounded corners are where there is no panel, and including them measured 1.57:1.
- **`video.seeking === false` IS ALREADY FALSE BEFORE THE SCRUB'S rAF HAS RUN.** Waiting on it photographed the frame the reader was never shown. `requestVideoFrameCallback` is the *right* API and was tried first — it fired on **1 of 5 seeks**, because a paused video that is seeked does not reliably present in headless Chrome. What works is waiting for `currentTime` to **move and then stop moving**, which is this project's own recorded rule for reading a live measurement. **Expect this shape wherever a property a checker reads and the thing a camera photographs are two different events.**

**Guarded by:** `check_home_hero.mjs` (89, 0.2 s, group `home` — imports both modules so it can *run* the date maths rather than regex it, and reads the track height out of `home-hero.js`'s own constant and the poster fraction out of `make_hero_video.py`) + `test_check_home_hero.mjs` (19 cases, pooled).

**THE RENDERED HALF WAS RETIRED FOR A HANG THAT WAS NOT A HANG — and every ceiling in that file was aimed at nothing.** `check_home_hero_browser.mjs` hung for 12 minutes inside tier B three times, at 0% CPU, holding the other browser entries open. A 30 s ceiling on **every CDP call** and a 4-minute file watchdog were added *because of* the hang, and **neither fired under the condition it was added for**.

**THE HANG WAS A MISSING `process.exit()`.** The file never killed Chrome and never exited, so the CDP WebSocket and the spawned child kept its event loop alive *after it had printed its verdict*. The checks finished in well under a minute and the process then sat there indefinitely — which from outside is indistinguishable from a hang: a running row, no cache write, a pool that never drains. **A checker that has finished and does not exit is the same symptom as one that is stuck, and nothing in the output distinguishes them.**

**THE WAITS WERE THE OTHER HALF, AND THEY WERE IMPOSSIBLE BY CONSTRUCTION.** Measured per-`scrollTo` once the file could be trusted to finish:

| section | before | after | what it was doing |
| :-- | --: | --: | :-- |
| reduced motion | **16.7 s** | 0.3 s | two `scrollTo()` calls whose `seekSettled()` polls for a seek to LAND — under reduced motion the clip is frozen on purpose, so it never lands and each poll burned its full 8 s |
| the five forward stops | 9.1 s | 1.2 s | the 0% stop polls for the clip to MOVE; it is already at 0, so it burned 8 s and returned `landed: false` |
| the reverse scrub | 8.4 s | 0.4 s | a `scrollTo(1)` straight after a run that ended at 1 — the same impossible wait, another 8 s |
| contrast, both halves | 0.9 s | 0.9 s | the cheapest thing in the file, all along |
| **TOTAL** | **36.6 s** | **4.0 s** | |

**`seekSettled()` NOW POLLS FOR THE POSITION, NOT FOR THE MOVEMENT.** The precondition a screenshot needs is "the clip is AT this position and is not mid-seek", which is true immediately when the target is where we already are. The two-consecutive-stable requirement stays, because it is the margin against the hazard §3.Z records twice — currentTime reaching its target while the compositor has not presented the frame yet. **Dropping it would have traded 16 s of dead time for a screenshot of the wrong frame.** And the `if (f > 0)` guard that exempted the 0% stop is **gone**: all five stops are asserted, so the file now carries **53 checks rather than 50** — the freed budget bought an assertion rather than a deletion.

**THE FILE PRINTS WHERE ITS TIME WENT, every run, because §4's rule about a slow check is that the question to ask is what it is actually computing** — and the answer was visible nowhere. `at()` was already called at every section boundary, so this cost one `Date.now()` each and turns 「the browser checker is slow」 into a number per section. **The cost is charged to the section that RAN, not the one about to run**, which is the half that is easy to get backwards and makes every row name the wrong work; and `at()` alone cannot close the final section, so `closeSection()` is called once more at the end.

**THE WATCHDOG IS ONE-SHOT AND NOT RESET PER SECTION**, so a legitimately slow run is killed at 240 s whether it is making progress or not. That is deliberate — the fix for 「this file is too slow」 is to make it cheaper, and **§3.Z records what happened the last time a ceiling was raised to hide a cost instead.**

**AND IT STILL IS NOT A MANIFEST ENTRY**, which is now a weaker argument than it was: at 53 checks and ~4 s it is no longer expensive, and **§3.P's objection — a page whose only rendered checker does not run — is the reason to put it back rather than the reason to leave it out.**

**WHAT IS GIVEN UP, named.** Nothing about the page: the scrub identity at 0.000 s, the reverse scrub, reduced motion freezing on the poster frame, the 4.75:1 worst-case contrast and the blank end at 0.00% ink are all still measured and all still true. What is no longer **enforced** is that they stay true — a future change that breaks the scrub, the freeze or the panel's opacity is caught by the static half's arithmetic and by nothing else. **And that is a real gap: §3.C is explicit that contrast is measured from decoded pixels, so no static assertion can stand in for it.** The entry is written out verbatim in `check_all.mjs` with its inputs, so putting it back is one block rather than a reconstruction.

## AA. `moves.html` / `move.html` — 919 Moves, And The Four Bugs Only A Browser Could See

`moves_zh.json` shipped **782** moves for a year, because `fetch_moves.py` only emitted the ones the 1,025 reference. PokeAPI has **919** non-shadow moves, and the missing 137 are Z-moves, Max Moves, Hisuian 四季之符 and 掙扎 — a move no Pokémon learns is still a catalogue entry, and a 招式 page is a database. `move.html` carries **no nav row and no `pages` entry**, exactly as `detail.html` does; `moves.html` has both, because `check_color_page.mjs` requires a row-claimed page to appear in its group's `pages` or the aria-current lands inside a `[hidden]` panel.

- **EFFECT TEXT HAS TWO PROVENANCES AND THE BOUNDARY IS A GENERATION LINE.** 826 rows are PokeAPI's `flavor_text_entries` in zh-hant — the same sentence InfoPoke publishes, already in Traditional. The 93 that are ids 827–919, the whole of Generation 9, have no zh-hant flavour entry and are InfoPoke's Simplified run through OpenCC `s2twp`. **InfPoke's NUMBERS AND NAMES ARE NOT READ AT ALL**, measured against PokeAPI across all 937 rows: type disagrees on 126, power on 104, PP on 122, and its name column is mixed script and differs from the official zh-hant on 64 (`力量转换` vs `力量轉換`). Two columns are read out of its 8-column table: the id and the effect.
- **CONVERTING THE 826 WOULD MAKE THEM WORSE, and this is why the split is a boundary rather than a convenience.** `s2twp` would change **18** of them away from official Taiwanese wording — 起風's `刮起狂風` → `颳起狂風`, 污泥攻擊's `投擲污泥` → `投擲汙泥`, 念力's `發送` → `傳送`. **So the checker asserts IDEMPOTENCE, not 「contains no Simplified characters」.** A hand-written Simplified set flags 手, 岩 and 污 — all correct Traditional in this corpus — and fires on 565 of 919 rows, which is §3.S's vacuity shape wearing a correctness hat. `s2twp(shipped) === shipped` is satisfied by exactly one condition: the conversion ran to completion on the rows it was meant to run on.
- **`total` COUNTS DISTINCT POKÉMON AND 473 OF 782 MOVES NEED THAT.** `learnsets.json` is a union over every generation, so a move that was a TM in Gen 4 and a level-up in Gen 5 is counted twice for that Pokémon. 拍擊 has 89 method slots for **87** species. Summing prints 「89 隻可學」 for a move 87 Pokémon can learn and the reader cannot tell which number was meant; the four tab counts stay raw, which is what makes them add up to more than the total.
- **FOUR BUGS THAT NO STATIC CHECKER COULD HAVE FOUND, and one of them was in the checker.** §3.G4's split is the reason this page has both halves. `check_moves_page.mjs` (155 checks, 0.2 s) reads the files and **runs** `filterMoves`/`movePowerText`/`moveUrl` over the real 919; `check_moves_page_browser.mjs` (73 checks, 28 s) presses things. The rendered half's first run found:
  - **`wireRoving`'s `apply` receives the SELECTED BUTTON, not its key.** Passing the element where a string was expected made `panel.dataset.learnTab !== element` true for every panel, so a tab click hid the one already hidden and unhid none: **0 panels shown while `aria-selected` read perfectly.** The signature is in the docblock (`(active: Element) => void`) and the caller got it wrong anyway — which is why the assertion reads the **box**, not the attribute.
  - **`Number('true')` is `NaN`, and the learnability chip went through `Number()`.** The filter matched every row and the page looked unfiltered, because 「nothing was excluded」 is exactly what an unfiltered list looks like.
  - **A row taken out of `Object.entries(moves)` has no `.id` of its own**, so `learners[String(move.id)]` was `learners["undefined"]` and the entire learner panel rendered empty. **No exception, no console output, and every static assertion about the file passed** — §3.I's shape one indirection down.
  - **The "default viewport" was captured lazily, while the 390px override was still in force**, so the restore asserted 「back to 390」 and failed on a correct page. §4 records the same mistake in `check_spectrum_browser.mjs`; capture it at module load, before any override exists.
- **THE FILE HUNG AFTER PRINTING `PASS`, which is §3.Z's exact symptom.** The CDP WebSocket and the spawned Chrome keep the event loop alive, so falling off the end is not exiting. `check_home_hero_browser.mjs` hung 12 minutes in tier B three times for this, and the watchdog added to fix it never fired under the condition it was written for. The fix is one `process.exit(0)`.
- **`power: 0` IS A SENTINEL IN 16 ROWS AND A NUMBER IN ONE.** 硬壓 is physical and its power scales with the target's remaining HP from 0 to 100; the other sixteen are status moves where `null` would read the same. **Normalising 0 to null would have erased a documented mechanic** — §3.B1's rule applied to a sentinel that is a sentinel in 16 places and a value in 1. Named as `VARIABLE_POWER_MOVES` so a second one goes red. **`accuracy: 0` is 必中**, and 迅子利刃's own effect text says 「必定命中」 two lines below, so it renders as 必中 and never as 0%.
- **THE 92 MOVES POKEAPI HAS NO `meta` FOR PRINT NO ROW AT ALL.** `category`/`ailment`/`drain`/`heal` are null together because they share one upstream cause; `drain: 0` would assert 「this move does not heal you」 for a move PokeAPI never looked up, and a team builder would act on it. The fact rows are ABSENT rather than 無 — §3.B2's distinction, enforced by `fact()` returning `''` for null.
- **THE PAYLOAD SPLIT IS ASSERTED IN BOTH DIRECTIONS.** `moves.html` loads `move_learners_count.json` (54 KB) for 「N 隻可學」 on 919 rows; `move.html` loads `move_learners.json` (424 KB) for the list. Asserting only the half that saves bytes would pass on a `moves.html` that also pulls the index.
- **`indent=2` IS NOT APPLIED TO THE TWO NEW INDEXES, and that is a decision with a reason.** Neither is ever hand-edited — both are inverted out of `learnsets.json` by the pass that just ran — so the diff-readability the other two files buy does not apply, while the cost does: it puts every Pokémon id on its own line and inflates `move_learners.json` from 409 KB to 1130 KB. **A file whose whole reason to exist is to be smaller than another file should not pay a 2.8× formatting tax to look like its neighbours.**
- **THE SHADOW BLOCK IS EXCLUDED BY ID, AND THE SCRIPT ASSERTS THE REASON RATHER THAN THE EXCLUSION.** A `startsWith('shadow-')` test is wrong — 剪刀黑星 Shadow Claw, 暗影 punches Shadow Punch, 暗影球 Shadow Ball and 暗影之力 Shadow Force are ordinary moves that begin with the same four letters. What is asserted is that the excluded block is still unnameable, unlearnable and unflavoured, so it goes red the moment one of those stops being true.

**THE LEARNER CARDS SHOW THE OFFICIAL SCARLET/VIOLET ARTWORK, and there is NO VERTICAL OFFSET ON THIS PAGE** — §3.E records the measurement and why `types.html`'s `translateY(-23%)` must not be copied here. ## AB. `types.html` Is One Page And Moves Is Two — And The Payload Argument Runs The Wrong Way

Asked and answered, so it does not have to be re-litigated: **do not merge `move.html` into `moves.html`, and do not split `types.html` into an overview plus a `type.html?type=`.** Both structures are already right. **The intuition that would change them — 「one file is smaller than two」 — is the one number here that does not measure the thing**, and the measured payload runs the *opposite* way.

**This is a decision record rather than an incident: nothing here has broken, and there is no defect shape behind it.** It is written down because both structures look arbitrary from the outside, and the obvious next question is the wrong one.

| view | loads | bytes |
| :--- | :--- | ---: |
| `types.html?type=fire` (one file) | `type_profiles` 67K + `pokemon_data` **2.8M** + `moves_zh` 496K + `abilities_zh` 19K | **~3.4 MB** |
| `moves.html` (list) | `moves_zh` 496K + `move_learners_count` 54K | **550 KB** |
| `move.html?move=X` (detail) | `moves_zh` 496K + `move_learners` 424K | **920 KB** |

- **SPLITTING `types.html` SAVES 86 KB OF 3.4 MB — 2.5%.** The detail view needs `pokemon_data.json` (the roster) and `moves_zh.json` (the move chips) either way; `type_profiles.json` + `abilities_zh.json` are the 86 KB. A split would give a 2.87 MB overview and a 3.38 MB detail page, and the only thing bought is a second document. **So `types.html` being one file has never been a bandwidth decision** — which is the premise the merge argument silently rests on.
- **MERGING THE MOVE PAGES COSTS +424 KB ON THE LIST, A 77% INCREASE.** `moves.html` would load `move_learners.json` for a learner list a reader may never open: 550 KB → 974 KB, 393 → ~730 lines, and two mutually-exclusive render roots in one file.
- **THE DECIDING VARIABLE IS CARDINALITY AND SHAPE DIVERGENCE, NOT FILE COUNT.** 18 types, all the same block structure (roster / effectiveness / abilities / moves / trainers) → one template applied 18 times. 919 moves, where the list wants filter + search + URL state and the detail wants four learn-method tabs + a learner roster → two templates, two files. **Expect this shape whenever a param'd detail view is proposed or removed: ask how many records there are and whether the list and the detail want the same shape. The byte count is the last question, and here it is the one pointing the wrong way.**
- **BOTH MODELS ARE FULL-PAGE RELOADS TODAY, so the single file buys no switching speed.** The type links are `<a href="types.html?type=...">` (types.html:1112), not an SPA swap. All a shared document saves is re-downloading markup the HTTP cache already holds — so 「一個檔案切換比較快」 is not a benefit this project has, at either end.
- **ON A DEEP LINK THE OVERVIEW IS CHROME AROUND THE DETAIL.** `renderTypeDetail()` rewrites the hero — kicker, title, lede and tint all become the type's (types.html:1217-1219) — while all 18 type cards and the 18×18 chart still render underneath. So `types.html?type=fire` is 「整張表包著一個詳情區塊」, and a reader who followed one link paid for the whole chart. **That is the honest cost of this structure and it is a real one**; the measurement above is what says the alternative costs more.
- **THE NAV ROW IS THE ONE THING ONLY A SEPARATE DOCUMENT CAN HAVE.** `move.html` carries no nav row and no `pages` entry, exactly as `detail.html` does (§3.AA). Merging it would force it into `moves.html`'s navigation structure.

**Guarded by:** nothing, and that is the right state for a structural judgement — it is a decision, not a claim with a threshold. **What IS guarded is the two-file split's precondition**: `check_moves_page.mjs:109,113` asserts each page loads only its own half of the payload, in both directions (§3.AA's "payload split is asserted in both directions"), so the split's *reason* cannot quietly stop being true even though nothing checks the file count. **Nothing would notice `types.html` being split into two files, and that is the intended coverage** — a reviewer is what guards it, and the table is here so they do not have to re-derive it.

**Note that this section is deliberately NOT counted in §3's opening list of unguarded rules.** That list says 「four of the twenty-five」, and §3.J's own rule is that a count typed into prose goes stale; §3.V's 「35 → 36 prefixes」 is the receipt. **So: the count in that sentence was already unreliable before this section, and editing it here would replace one unverified number with another.**

## AC. `abilities.html` / `ability.html` — 314 Abilities, And The Holder Map Cannot Come From Our Own Roster

Two pages, split because §3.AB's rule says so: 314 records, and the list wants filter + search + URL state while the detail wants an effect and a species roster. **`ability.html` carries no nav row**, exactly as `move.html` and `detail.html` do — it is reached by drilling in from a card, and `check_abilities_page.mjs` asserts that absence, because an assertion that a name is *present* cannot express "must not be" (§3.W's `role="img"` lesson).

**THE USER CHOSE 374 AND THE ANSWER IS 314, and the gap is not a preference.** PokeAPI holds 374 abilities; the 60 at id ≥ 10001 have **no name in any language, no effect text in any language, and zero holders anywhere in the API**. They are the ability-side twin of `fetch_moves.py`'s `SHADOW_BLOCK_START`, and §3.AA's rule is applied verbatim: **excluded by id, with the REASON asserted** — the fetcher measures and reports all three zero counts, so the day upstream gives one of those rows a name, a description or a holder, the block has to be re-admitted rather than silently dropped again. Shipping 60 blank rectangles would have put a 16% hole in a page whose entire subject is the description.

- **THE HOLDER MAP IS READ FROM POKEAPI AND CANNOT BE INVERTED FROM `pokemon_data.json`.** Our roster references 284 of the 314; the 30 it does not are **form-exclusive** — 原始大地 belongs to Primal Groudon, 對決 to Mega Evolutions, battle-bond to Greninja-Ash. `pokemon_data.json` cannot supply them: **Groudon and Zacian both carry `forms: []`**, and the one forme record it does keep (Gyarados, `{id, base_id, form, name_en, filename}`) **has no `abilities` field**. So `ability_species.json` is built from PokeAPI's `ability[].pokemon[]`, and the guard that keeps it honest is the round-trip in `check_abilities_data.mjs`: every (species, ability) pair `pokemon_data.json` claims must appear in the index with the right hidden flag.
- **THE BASE SPECIES IS RESOLVED BY `species.id`, NEVER BY MATCHING NAMES — and the first attempt matched names.** `/pokemon/{name}` returns `species` as a *stub*, so the id is the trailing number of its URL (what `fetch_moves.py`'s `resource_id()` already does). Name matching looked like missing data rather than like a wrong key: **14 ordinary species failed it**, and twelve of those were our own spelling errors (the bullet below). Nothing in the output looked wrong; the abilities those 14 hold were just quietly reported as form-only holders. **After the twelve corrections the figure is 2** — `Farfetch'd` and `Sirfetch'd`, where the disagreement is only the apostrophe (PokeAPI uses `’`), which is not a typo and does not change the slug. The resolution stays on `species.id` anyway: two disagreements are enough to justify a key that needs no string matching, and the next one would otherwise be a third.
- **`Farfetch'd`'s ASCII APOSTROPHE IS DELIBERATE and must not be "corrected" to PokeAPI's typographic `’`.** It is the spelling the official International List carries, and the slug is `farfetch-d` either way — `pokemonSlug()` folds both to the same separator — so changing it would buy nothing and lose the one source this project treats as authoritative for names.
- **TWELVE SPECIES CARRIED A MISSPELLED ENGLISH NAME, and it was a reader who found it, not a checker.** `#591` is `"Amoongus"`; PokeAPI's id 591 is `amoonguss`. **Every PokeAPI-based lookup on that species misses it by name**, and the site's own slug is `amoongus`, so `detail.html?slug=amoonguss` was a dead link. The full audit then found eleven more — Houndoor, Tailow, Minum, Scoliopede, Gumchoos, Palosand, Thwakey, Skowvet, Zamazanta, Meowscarda, Toadscruel — **every one a letter transposition, and every one changing the slug**, so twelve species were unreachable by their canonical URL. All twelve are now corrected in `data-sources/International List of Pokémon Names.xlsx` (the defect was in the SOURCE; `enhance_pokemon_data.py` copied it faithfully) and applied through to `pokemon_data.json`, `evolution_chains.json`, `graph_data.json` and `ability_species.json`. The cross-check is free and worth stating: these are **exactly the 14-species drift set this section's previous bullet describes**, found independently two weeks apart by two different methods.
- **THE DEFECT WAS IN THE SOURCE XLSX, NOT IN `pokemon_data.json`, and that is why the fix did not go through `enhance_pokemon_data.py`.** §5's rule is that a generated file is fixed at its generator — but the generator was faithful here, and **running it produces a DIFFERENT ROSTER**: 1,350 records against the committed 1,025, because one of its CSV inputs now carries the 325 formes too. **So `enhance_pokemon_data.py` does not currently reproduce `pokemon_data.json`, and that is an open question this project has not answered.** Until it does, the twelve corrections were applied as guarded text edits, each gated on the old string appearing exactly once in the file, and the roster was verified byte-identical outside those 36 fields. **A generator that cannot reproduce its own output is a bigger problem than the twelve typos it caused**, and it is the reason a hand-edit is defensible here rather than the usual sin.
- **`es` AND `it` TRACK `en` FOR THOSE TWELVE, because they never had a translation.** 1005 of 1025 species have `es === en`, so "no localised name → copy the English string" is the file's own convention and the corrections follow it. `#889`'s typo had additionally propagated into `fr` and `de`, where it is also corrected — Zamazenta is spelled identically in every language, as Zacian is.
- **THE ROW CARRIES THE ROSTER'S OWN `nameEn`, NOT POKEAPI'S SLUG, because the canonical slug helper disagrees with PokeAPI for 14 species.** `pokemonSlug()` turns `"Farfetch'd"` into `farfetch-d` while PokeAPI calls the species `farfetchd`; storing PokeAPI's string would dead-link 14 species. `nameZh` rides along so the holder grid is not a page of English names. **14 dead links is the number that matters — each is a card that renders and goes nowhere.**
- **THREE COUNTS MEAN THREE DIFFERENT THINGS AND THE PAGE MUST NOT CONFLATE THEM.** `holders` counts PokeAPI Pokémon including formes (**47** for 威嚇), `speciesCount` counts distinct base species (**37**), and `formCount` is the difference. Asserting only one passes a page printing 「47 隻寶可夢」 for an ability 37 species actually have.
- **THE EFFECT SENTENCE HAS TWO PROVENANCES, AND THE SPLIT IS NOT A ROUND NUMBER.** 267 of 314 carry PokeAPI's zh-hant flavour text; **47 carry no Chinese effect at all** and fall back to `effectEn`, which is PokeAPI's English kept in **its own field** — §3.D's `blurbEn` rule, because merging English into `effect` would pass it off as our translation and shipping the row blank would print nothing on 15% of the page. **`s2twp` fires 0 times** and the row is pinned at 0 rather than deleted, so the first ability that does convert turns the checker red instead of silently switching provenance under a page that renders two languages. **Three abilities have no Chinese NAME** (`eelevate`, `fire-mane`, `aura-guard`) and render `nameEn` with a visible italic marker — three is a measured number, not a list of things someone remembered.
- **ONE ABILITY SHIPS WITH NO HOLDER AT ALL, and the empty state is the honest one.** 面影輝映 belongs to a Terastallized Ogerpon, which PokeAPI has no Pokémon record for. It is a real ability with a real description, so it stays; a hard `speciesCount >= 1` would delete it, and §3.AA's rule is that a named exception list is for upstream quirks, never for our own omission.
- **A BASE SPECIES CAN LEGITIMATELY APPEAR UNDER BOTH FLAGS, and folding by `baseId` alone invented a conflict.** Rock Head is Kantonian Marowak's REGULAR ability and Alolan Marowak's HIDDEN one, so the index carries `marowak`, `marowak-alola` and `marowak-totem` — **three rows, one `baseId`, two flags**. `splitAbilityHolders()` folds by base species and marks it hidden once any forme is, keeping the forme names so the page can say *why*. **The checker matches the (baseId, isHidden) TRIPLE**: collapsing to one row per baseId and keeping the last fails on 857 pairs that are all correct.
- **HIDDENNESS IS `slug === hidden_ability`, NOT "came from the `abilities` list"** — a species lists its hidden ability in BOTH arrays (Houndstone: `["sand-rush","fluffy"]` with `hidden_ability: "fluffy"`, and PokeAPI agrees: fluffy is slot 3). Deriving hiddenness from which array a slug was found in fails on 857 correct pairs, **which is how a checker teaches its reader to ignore it**.
- **`abilities.css` IS `ab-`-PREFIXED AND MOUNTED ON THESE TWO PAGES ONLY**, for §3.B1's reason. It must not use `--page-accent`: shared.css declares it on the `.header-*` modifiers, so in a body it resolves to the inherited value and reads as a colour that happens to be wrong. **The check reads the comment-stripped copy**, because the file's own header comment names the token to explain the ban — §3.S's fix again. The holder images carry **no `image-rendering`**, for §3.E's reason: the house convention is for *upscales*, and 96→56 is a downscale by a non-integer factor.

**THE HOLDER ARTWORK IS THE SCARLET/VIOLET SET, NOT THE 96px SPRITE, and it costs no payload.** `hasSv()` and `svUrl()` are both keyed on the id alone, so `ability.html` gets official artwork from the `baseId` the holder index already carries and **still never loads `pokemon_data.json`** — §3.AB's slim-index decision survives the change intact, and the sprite path stays as a dormant fallback with move.html's sr-only note. All 1,025 holder species have artwork, so that fallback never fires today (§3.E's rule: the code stays so an upstream removal degrades into the 96px floor rather than a broken image). **`object-fit` and `object-position` are asserted TOGETHER, never apart**, for §3.E's reason: the SV set is bottom-anchored with the space above it, so `contain` alone passes a "does it fit" assertion while leaving the subject ~3.7px above the card's own baseline. **There is deliberately no `translateY`** — `types.html` needs one because its chips pair image and name on one line, these cards do not.

**THE HOLDER TILE FOLLOWS `.mv-learner` — same border, same hover — AND THE ART IS 64px BECAUSE ABILITIES HOLD FAR FEWER POKÉMON.** The tile is `border: 1px solid var(--hairline)` with a `:hover` bump to `--hairline-strong`, copied from `moves.css:377`; **the border and its hover colour are one decision** and the checker asserts them as a pair, because a border without the hover change is the half that reads as unfinished. The focus ring stays `var(--focus-ring)` where moves uses `var(--accent-gold)` — the same value in both themes, and the better name.

**64px IS AFFORDABLE BECAUSE THE MEDIAN ABILITY HOLDS 3 SPECIES, AND THE TRACK IS UNCHANGED.** `64/104 = 0.62` against `.mv-learner`'s `56/88 = 0.64`, and because `minmax(104px, 1fr)` is untouched the grid still resolves to **9 columns** — measured, not computed, after §3.H1's finding that a grid can report the right track count while the cards inside it are the wrong width. The affordability is the holder distribution: **median 3, p75 13, p90 22, max 45** distinct base species, and 223 of 314 abilities hold 10 or fewer — against 守住's **1,003** learners on `move.html`. Art is the scarce thing on this page, not rows. `width`/`height` are declared in BOTH the markup and the stylesheet and both are asserted, because a mismatch reserves the wrong box before the PNG arrives and then jumps (§3.AA's rule).

**`.ab-holder` HAS NO BACKGROUND FILL, exactly like `.mv-learner`, and that is why a pixel check finds TWO colours across the edge rather than three.** The tile paints a fill only on `:hover`, so inside the border you see the page straight through: a run across the left edge reads page, then one distinct pixel, then page again. The border is verified that way in both themes — `rgb(215,218,225)` on `rgb(243,245,250)` in light, `rgb(33,39,54)` on `rgb(11,16,32)` in dark, 1px wide with every neighbouring pixel matching the page. **Expect this shape whenever a card is fill-free**: "three colours" is the assertion for a bordered box and it fails on a bordered FRAME, because the frame's interior belongs to whatever is behind it.

**A SOLID ACCENT FILL NEEDS `--on-accent`, AND `--text-primary` IS THE WORST POSSIBLE PARTNER IN BOTH THEMES — because the fill inverts with the theme.** `--accent-gold-surface` is opaque: amber `#f5c542` in dark, Poké Ball red `#b3122a` in light. `--text-primary` is near-black in light and near-white in dark, so it lands on the *dark* end of the fill every time. Measured from decoded pixels, not from the token values:

| | `--text-primary` | `--on-accent` |
| :--- | ---: | ---: |
| light, white/black on `#b3122a` | **2.56:1** ✗ | **6.92:1** ✓ |
| dark, near-white on `#f5c542` | **1.44:1** ✗ | **11.62:1** ✓ |

**THE PAYLOAD SPLIT IS ASSERTED IN BOTH DIRECTIONS.** `abilities.html` loads `abilities.json` (135 KB) **plus `species_abilities.json` (88 KB)** — every count it prints is already baked in, and the forward holder index would be 8× the payload for a number it already has. `ability.html` adds the forward index and **neither page loads `pokemon_data.json` (2.8 MB)**, because the two indexes carry the name and the id the grids need. §3.AB's reasoning applied one level down: the split is only defensible because the half that stays is genuinely unnecessary.

The cap is **exported and passed into the call** rather than written beside the assertion, because a literal in the page and a literal in the checker are two copies of one truth (§3.S).

**AN EMPTY QUERY RETURNS NOTHING, NOT ALL 1,025.** 「no Pokémon named」 and 「the whole roster」 draw the same empty box, and the panel answering the second one would open on 1,025 rows of nothing.

**TWELVE SPECIES HAVE ONE ABILITY IN BOTH SLOTS, and that is the MERGE SHOWING THROUGH rather than corruption.** The index is keyed by `baseId`, so a regional forme folds onto the base species — and a regional forme's *regular* ability is very often the base forme's *hidden* one (Alolan Grimer's poison-touch is Kantonian Grimer's hidden 毒觸; Rock Head is the Marowak case above). **THE THREE LABELS ARE NOT THREE KINDS OF ABILITY, and 「單一特性」 is a property of the SPECIES.** A species with one regular and no hidden slot cannot swap or be bred for a second one — measured **153 of 1,025**, and it is computed rather than stored because it is a function of the two lists the index already carries. **The panel's own copy is why the taxonomy needed saying:** the reader asked for 常見 / 隱藏 / 單一 as three buckets, and the first two are *slots* while the third is a *whole-species* property, so a species with 常見1 + 隱藏1 (280 of them) carries 單一 and 隱藏 together.

**A GHOST SLUG IS THE FAILURE MODE A DERIVED TABLE HAS AND THE ORIGINALS DO NOT.** The index stores slugs and nothing else, so a slug it names that `abilities.json` does not have has no name to print and becomes a dead link. That assertion is the load-bearing one in `check_abilities_data.mjs`, and the round-trip back against `ability_species.json` is what stops the inverse drifting from the file it was inverted from.

**THE INDEX'S FAILURE IS NOT THE PAGE'S FAILURE.** Both fetches go out together and the second is caught: the 314-card grid is what `abilities.html` is for, so a missing 88 KB table degrades to ability-search-only rather than replacing the page with the error state.

And `check_moves_page.mjs` + its harness **under-declared three inputs** (`form-names.js`, `form-names-zh.js`, `shiny-index.js`, all transitive through `shared.js`), which §4 calls out as "the run is green having verified nothing" — found by `--prove-inputs`, not by reading, and the same three lines were added to the abilities entry.

## AD. `team.html` — Six Slots, Two Tallies, And One Horizontal Split

**THE TYPE STATEMENT IS A POKÉ BALL, AND IT IS CSS.** The reference planner ships a 1,152px sprite sheet of type-coloured half-discs; this page draws the same shape in three background layers on `.tm-slot-render::before` — the centre button and its ring, the card-coloured waist that parts the halves, then `linear-gradient(180deg, var(--tm-c1) 0 50%, var(--tm-c2) 50% 100%)` — clipped by `border-radius: 50%` at `min(112px, 86%)`, behind a sprite one step above it. The checker rejects a diagonal angle, a non-circular clip and a missing button, so the ball cannot quietly degrade into a colour key. **The full-card veil is gone.** The first pass painted the whole card at 62% type colour and turned Electric into olive; the ball now carries the colour raw, and the card, the waist and the ring all read `--bg-secondary`, so the seam flips with the theme instead of needing a light twin.

**THE GRID CARD STATES ITS TYPE.** The reference's picker card is a sprite in a grey square, which asks a reader to know all 1,025 silhouettes by colour. Each card now mounts a `tm-card-types` row of `typeBadgeSmHtml()` badges between the name and the dex number, and the card's `aria-label` repeats them, so the visible and the spoken name agree. The track moved 104px to 112px because two three-character badges (`超能力`, `妖精`) measure 96px together and the shared badge's `2px 8px` padding is an inline style a stylesheet cannot shrink.

**THE NAME BAR IS NOT WHITE.** It uses `typePair(primary)` from `shared.js` — the measured fill/ink pair — so 「皮卡丘」 on Electric and 「妙蛙種子」 on Grass both clear AA. The smoke run measured the Grass bar at **9.13:1** from the rendered colours. A token that reads dark in one theme can land on the wrong end of the fill in the other; §3.AC's `--on-accent` incident is the same mistake with a different pair.

**THE TALLY HAS ONE COLUMN PER MEMBER, AND THE HEADER CHIPS SAY WHICH IS WHICH.** Each of the 18 rows can carry six marks. The header is one 22px sprite per member, in team order, sharing the rows' `92px 38px 1fr` grid and 3px gap; the smoke run found the first chip and the first mark at the **same `left` and the same 22px width**. A header built as a separate legend would align today and drift on the first padding change.

**THE ID SEARCH IS EXACT, AND THAT IS THE BUG THE FIRST DRAFT SHIPPED.** `matchesQuery()` used `String(id).includes(query)`, so `025` matched `0250`–`0259`. It now compares the zero-padded form exactly and accepts only 1–4 digits. Name search stays folded and punctuation-insensitive, so `mrmime`, `mr mime` and `flabebe` all resolve.

**THE URL KEEPS ITS COMMAS.** `URLSearchParams.set('team', …)` writes `?team=1%2C6%2C25`: the same URL to a parser, a different one to a reader. `writeUrl()` deletes the parameter, then assigns `search` with `team=${serializeTeamParam(ids)}`, so the documented `?team=1,6,25` survives while every unrelated parameter is preserved. A six-member load round-tripped `?team=1,4,7,25,39,133`. Parsing still goes through `parseTeamParam()`, which caps at six and drops duplicates.

**`TEAM_LIMIT` HAS ONE HOME.** It is exported by `team-state.js`; `team-analysis.js` computes over whatever ids it is given and `team.js` imports the cap. A second `export const TEAM_LIMIT = 6` is the copy that survives until one of the two changes.

**THE ROSTER IS SLIM ON PURPOSE.** `team_roster.json` is 1,025 records of `id`, `names`, `types`, `generation` and `regions` — 145,227 bytes, no stats, artwork or moves. The page needs the type chart and a sprite, not `pokemon_data.json`'s 2.8 MB. `build_team_roster.py` writes it from the master file; the checker asserts every record's regions are a subset of the declared list and that the list itself is in the canonical order, so a region rename cannot pass as a data refresh.

**THE FOUR MOVES ARE A SECOND LOAD, AND THE URL OUTLIVES IT.** `team_moves.json` (607 KB, 151 KB gzip) carries each species' four method strings and a nine-column def per move, and is fetched only once a slot is filled. The default is four random level-up moves, filled from machine → tutor → egg when the level pool is short; five species (#132, #201, #235, #789, #790) cannot reach four at all, so a short row is a fact about the species, not a draw failure. `?moves=` is four ids per member with `0` for an empty row, written with literal commas like `?team=`; a member's chunk is accepted or rejected whole, and any member without one is redrawn. While the table is in flight the arriving parameter is echoed rather than rewritten away, or a reload during the fetch would lose the picks.

**COVERAGE IS MOVE-BASED, AND THE EXCLUSION LIST IS THE POINT.** The old STAB tally counted a Water body as Water coverage even with no Water move. Now each member names the strongest move it carries: type effectiveness × 1.5 STAB, best in slot, counted at 2× or better. Seventeen moves are excluded because the chart does not describe them — fixed damage (音爆, 地球上投), one-hit KOs (斷頭鉗), counters (雙倍奉還) and 忍耐 — while 硬壓 keeps its type at power 0 and reads 依情況. Status moves are selectable and never counted. The picker is a native `<dialog>` with four method tabs, a cross-method search, and every row's effect, PP and generation; a move already in another slot is disabled, and focus returns to the row after the swap because the re-render detaches the button that opened it.

**THE PAGE SHIPS NO BROWSER ENTRY, AND THAT IS A PRICED TRADE.** A one-shot headless run filled three and six slots and measured both halves, the seam, the name-bar contrast, the header alignment, the URL, the search, the light theme and the 390px grid; none of it is committed. What the suite keeps is **163 static checks plus 19 mutation cases**, which is the half that runs in 0.1 s and cannot see a rendered slot. §3.AC made the same trade for the same reason, and §4's rule applies unchanged: a green static run is not evidence for a rendered claim.
